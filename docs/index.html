Privacy Policy for SpeedSync

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Last updated: September 8, 2026

SpeedSync ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by memamun. We value your privacy and are committed to ensuring your personal data is protected. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled by the SpeedSync application.

1. Summary: No Personal Data Collected

SpeedSync is built strictly as an on-device network telemetry and utility tool:

  • We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal data.
  • We do NOT use external analytics, ads, tracking libraries, or third-party profiling SDKs.
  • All network calculations and daily data counters are computed and stored 100% locally on your device.

2. Android Permissions and Purpose of Use

Speed Meter requests minimal permissions required exclusively to perform its core network speed meter and status bar telemetry functions:

  • Foreground Service (Data Sync) (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): Used solely to run the persistent, low-power background service that calculates real-time download and upload transfer rates and presents live status bar updates.
  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Under Android operating system policy, accessing the connected Wi-Fi router name (SSID) requires location permission. Speed Meter accesses this permission strictly on-device to identify and show your Wi-Fi name in the app header and notification panel. Your physical GPS coordinates are never accessed, tracked, recorded, or transmitted.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to display the ongoing silent speed telemetry notification and status bar icon.
  • Start at Boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): If enabled by you in Settings, allows the background meter to automatically resume when you restart your phone.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Optional user-prompted permission to prevent Android from aggressively killing the speed meter service while the screen is on.

3. Data Storage & Retention

Daily and monthly data usage records are stored exclusively within the application's local private sandbox (using Android SharedPreferences). Uninstalling the application completely deletes all local usage statistics from your device.

4. Third-Party Services

Speed Meter does not transmit any of your data to third-party servers, advertisers, or cloud providers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
