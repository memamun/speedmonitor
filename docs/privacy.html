Privacy Policy for SpeedSync

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 • Last updated: September 8, 2026

SpeedSync ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source Android application developed by memamun. We respect and value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our strict practices regarding data protection and device permissions.

1. Zero Data Collection & Transmission

SpeedSync is architected purely as an on-device utility and network telemetry monitor:

  • We do NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.
  • We do NOT integrate external analytics, advertising libraries, tracking SDKs, or profiling tools.
  • All network transfer throughput calculations, ping latency diagnostics, and daily data counters are processed and retained 100% locally inside your device's isolated application sandbox.

2. Android Permissions & Purpose of Use

SpeedSync requests only the strictly required permissions necessary to deliver its status bar speed meter and data monitoring features:

  • Foreground Service (Data Sync) (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC): Required to operate the low-latency background monitoring service that reads local network traffic statistics (via Android TrafficStats) and renders updates to the status bar and notification drawer.
  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Required by Android OS policies starting in Android 9+ to read the connected Wi-Fi router name (SSID). SpeedSync accesses this permission strictly on-device so it can display your Wi-Fi name in the app and notification. Your physical GPS coordinates are NEVER accessed, tracked, logged, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required on Android 13+ to render the ongoing silent speed notification and status bar indicator.
  • Start at Boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): If enabled in Settings, allows the background speed service to automatically start when your device reboots.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS): Optional permission prompted to ensure uninterrupted speed monitoring without the Android OS prematurely killing the background service.

3. Local Storage & Data Retention

Daily and monthly network usage totals are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences on your device. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling SpeedSync immediately and permanently purges all stored statistics.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

SpeedSync contains no third-party telemetry services. If you visit external links (such as our GitHub repository or project website), their respective privacy policies apply.

5. Children's Privacy

Because SpeedSync does not collect, retain, or transmit any data whatsoever, it is safe for all users and complies with global child privacy regulations including COPPA.
